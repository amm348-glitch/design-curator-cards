# CLAIMS Verification Log

## Card 1 — Bauhaus (1919–1933)
**Claim:** The Bauhaus was founded by Walter Gropius and closed in 1933.  
**Source:** https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/bauhaus  
**Verification:** The MoMA Bauhaus term page lists the founding year (1919), closure year (1933), and identifies Walter Gropius as the founder.  
**Confidence:** High

## Card 2 — Swiss Style / International Typographic Style (1950s–1970s)
**Claim:** Swiss Style emphasizes grid systems, objectivity, and typographic clarity.  
**Source:** https://www.moma.org/collection/artists/4154  
**Verification:** Josef Müller-Brockmann’s MoMA artist record describes his grid-based, objective approach to visual communication.  
**Confidence:** Medium–High

## Card 3 — New Wave Typography (1970s–1980s)
**Claim:** Wolfgang Weingart is associated with experimental typography that reacted against strict Swiss Style conventions.  
**Source:** https://www.moma.org/collection/artists/6289  
**Verification:** The MoMA artist record connects Weingart to experimental Swiss typography and post–Swiss Style approaches.  
**Confidence:** Medium
